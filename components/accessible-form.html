Component: Accessible form

Pattern: every control has a real <label for>, related controls are grouped in a <fieldset> with a <legend>, required fields carry both the native required attribute and aria-required="true" (for older assistive tech), format hints use pattern/ minlength plus a visible .hint tied in with aria-describedby, and radio/checkbox groups sit inside their own nested <fieldset>.
